Face & habits

A puffy face is usually fat plus water retention. These habits reduce it quickly while the fat comes off everywhere.

Today's habits

What actually helps

  • Cut sodium. Skip noodle/hotpot broth, use half the sauce. The biggest cause of morning puffiness.
  • No sweet drinks or alcohol. They drive water retention and add hundreds of calories.
  • Sleep 7–8 hours. Short sleep raises cortisol, which holds water and fat.
  • Drink 2.5–3 L of water. Counter-intuitively it reduces retention.
  • Be patient. Face fat usually shows after about 4–6 kg lost. Compare photos every 2 weeks.
