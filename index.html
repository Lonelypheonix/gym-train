Face & daily habits

A puffy face is usually fat plus water retention. You cannot burn fat from the face directly, but the fat comes off everywhere as your overall body fat drops, and these habits reduce the puffiness quickly.

Today's habits

What actually helps

  • Cut sodium. Do not drink noodle or hotpot broth, use half the sauce, skip instant noodles. Salt is the biggest cause of morning puffiness in Taiwanese food.
  • No sweet drinks or alcohol. Bubble tea, juice and alcohol drive water retention and add hundreds of calories. Sugar-free tea or black coffee before 14:00 is fine.
  • Sleep 7 to 8 hours. Short sleep raises cortisol, which holds water and fat.
  • Drink 2.5 to 3 L of water. Counter-intuitively it reduces retention.
  • Be patient. Face fat usually shows change after about 4 to 6 kg lost. Compare photos every 2 weeks, not daily.
